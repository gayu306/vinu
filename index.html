Vinu, this is for you.

You’ve officially unlocked
a tiny piece of my heart.

Don't skip. I made this just for you. 🫶🏻

Somehow… it became us.

I don't even know when you went from being just you to becoming such a big part of my little world.

Okay, let's be honest… 😂

We are NOT the calm, peaceful, perfect couple from those cute Instagram reels. 😭

We fight. We irritate each other. We overthink. We annoy each other for absolutely no reason.

And somehow… we still end up laughing together. ❤️

But then… 🥹

There are days when everything feels easy.

Just being next to you. Laughing at stupid things. Taking random pictures. Doing absolutely nothing and still having a good time.

And those little moments? Those are my favourite.

🫶🏻

The truth.

We've had our ups. We've definitely had our downs.

We've misunderstood each other, had silly fights, and had moments when things weren't easy.

But through every version of us…

I still find my way back to you.

My favourite part.

Maybe that's what I love most about us.

Not that we're perfect.

But even after the bad days, the silly fights, the misunderstandings and all the drama…

I still look at you and think,

“Yep. It's you.” ❤️

If I had to choose…

If life gave me the choice to go back and start everything again…

I'd still want the same laughs.
The same stupid conversations.
The same memories.
The same annoying boy. 😭

And somehow,
I'd still end up with you.

Every. Single. Time. ❤️

More us. 🤍

Vinu,

I don't know what the future is going to look like. I don't know how many more ups and downs we'll have.

But I know one thing…

I want to keep making memories with you.

More pictures. More laughs. More fights we'll eventually laugh about. More random adventures.

More us.

❤️

Happy Boyfriend's Day, Vinu.

Thank you for being you.

For making me laugh. For being a part of my life. For giving me memories I'll always keep close.

And for somehow becoming my favourite person to annoy. 😂

I love you, idiot. 🫶🏻

— Your girl ❤️
